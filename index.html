The Buyer Behind the Profile

Kishore's career tells a story of someone who keeps showing up and figuring things out, no matter how unfamiliar the situation. Early on, he managed a 150-person construction team on refinery and petrochemical projects, coordinating crews, schedules, and start-ups. Later, he built an entirely new commercial capability inside a major energy company, growing it from zero to a five-person team and creating a training program that ran for seven years across five countries.

He has negotiated with governments, managed joint ventures, and led sourcing efforts that saved real money. But he has also gotten his hands dirty. Kishore co-founded a small wellness business where he handled everything from hiring to marketing to daily operations. That experience showed him exactly what he loves about running a business and where he wants help.

Over the past year, Kishore has been searching full-time. He has gone deep on several opportunities, walked operations, built roadmaps alongside owners, and submitted offers. He is not browsing. He is doing the work.

Rooted in Katy with his wife and two kids, Kishore is active in his community. He serves as president of his neighborhood HOA, co-facilitates a CEO roundtable, advises small businesses through Silver Fox Advisors, and volunteers with a local homeless shelter. He wants a business that lets him be present for his family, take care of employees, and build something he is proud of.

Vision: Kishore wants to step into a business that already works and build on what is already there, bringing calm leadership, strong systems, and a genuine commitment to the people who made this business successful. His goal is to protect what the seller built, create growth, and carry the business forward in a way his family and employees can feel proud of. At its heart, this is also personal. Kishore wants to show his children that following your convictions and betting on what you believe in ultimately pays off.
